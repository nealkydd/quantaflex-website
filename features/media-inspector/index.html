READ THE FILE, NOT THE LABEL

An .mp4 tells you almost nothing.

Two files can both end in .mp4 and behave completely differently. One may be ready for a social upload; another may have the wrong frame rate, a legacy codec, unsupported colour, or audio that will not survive the next stage of the workflow.

01

Container

MP4, MOV, MKV, and WebM are wrappers. The report identifies the file format instead of asking you to guess from its extension.

02

Video codec

See whether the image stream is H.264/AVC, H.265/HEVC, VP9, AV1, ProRes, or another codec — and make the right next decision.

03

Audio codec

Check AAC, PCM, MP3, Opus, or other audio streams alongside sample rate, channel layout, and bitrate.

04

Delivery facts

Resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, duration, total size, and overall bitrate are all in one useful view.
