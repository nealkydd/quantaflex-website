DIRECT COMMUNICATION

CAM2CAM

Private video sessions, kept focused.

Bring camera, microphone, screen sharing, chat, background effects, recording and layout controls into one clear desktop workspace. Join with a room code, then keep the session focused.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

A clear workspace before the call begins.

Check camera, microphone and background before starting. Choose Start Call to host, or Join Call and enter the room code from your invitation.

GROUP VIDEO CALLS

See everyone together.

Cam2Cam brings up to 5 people in total into one video call: one host and four guests. The All layout keeps everyone visible, with call booking and invitation emails available from Video Call Scheduling.

VIDEO CALL SCHEDULING

Arrange the call. Send the invitation.

From the home screen, choose Video Call Scheduling to book a Cam2Cam call. Review the invitation with its time, room code and joining instructions, then send it through Gmail, Outlook or your mail app.

Joining someone else’s call?

Use the room code in their invitation: open Cam2Cam, press Start, then Join Call. For booked calls, the green room opens 10 minutes before the start.

Quantaflex’s calendar keeps the bookings you create on that computer. Received invitations can be added to Google Calendar; they do not automatically appear in Quantaflex’s own calendar.
