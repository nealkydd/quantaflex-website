PRESENT & TEACH

PRO ONLINE LEARNING

Teach, present, and share from one focused workspace.

Teach one-to-one or bring together one presenter and up to six learners. Present PowerPoint, a PDF, an image, or a screen or window you choose. Keep your presenter notes and next-slide preview separate from the view learners receive.

SESSION READY

A calm starting point for every lesson.

Set up the presenter camera and shared material before learners join. In the green room, learners check their camera, microphone and background, then wait for the host to admit them to the stage.

GROUP TUTORIALS

One presenter. Six learners.

Keep the presenter and all six learners visible together while your shared material has its own space. Named participant tiles make a small group easier to follow.

PREPARE & INVITE

A booked session, with room to prepare.

Open Video Call Scheduling from the home screen, choose Pro Online Learning, and set the time and guests. Review the invitation before sending it through Gmail, Outlook or your mail app. The green room opens 10 minutes before the booked start.

Your presenter view stays private.

For PowerPoint, share the slideshow window rather than your whole desktop to keep notes and next-slide previews private. For a PDF or image, press Start Presenting to share the document view. Play Sound sends an audio file to the session.

Joining as a learner

Open Pro Online Learning, press Start, then Join Call and enter the room code from your invitation. Wait in the green room until the host admits you. Keep a working camera on while you are on stage.
