Cam2Cam uses a room code for joining. The host starts the session and copies the code; the other person enters it to join. The conversation begins with less account friction and a clearer sense of who is in the room.

Camera, microphone, screen sharing, text chat, background effects and layout controls stay together in the same workspace. Screen sharing uses its own channel, keeping the conversation and the shared material legible.
