Start the session, then share one code.

Cam2Cam uses a room code for joining. The host starts the session and copies the code; guests enter it to join. A call can include up to five people in total: one host and four guests. The conversation begins with less account friction and a clearer sense of who is in the room.

Keep the work in the call.

Camera, microphone, screen sharing, text chat, background effects and layout controls stay together in the same workspace. Screen sharing uses its own channel, keeping the conversation and the shared material legible.

Keep the recording local.

When you record a session, the file is saved on your own computer. The connection is encrypted and Quantaflex does not retain copies of your calls.

Arrange a call ahead of time.

From the home screen, open Video Call Scheduling, choose Cam2Cam, and set the time and guests. Review the invitation with its room code and joining instructions, then send it through Gmail, Outlook or your mail app.

For booked calls, the green room opens 10 minutes before the start. Guests open Cam2Cam, press Start, then Join Call and enter the room code. The All layout keeps all five people visible together.

Quantaflex’s calendar keeps bookings you create on that computer. Received invitations can be added to Google Calendar; they do not automatically appear in Quantaflex’s own calendar.
