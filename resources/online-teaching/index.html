Useful questions before you teach online.

What should I prepare before an online lesson?

Prepare the teaching material, test the presenter camera and audio, and check what the learner will see. A short run-through of the opening minute removes most avoidable disruption.

How can I present slides while keeping private notes?

Pro Online Learning extracts PowerPoint notes into a private presenter workspace while learners see the clean shared slide.

Can an online teaching workflow be permanent software rather than another subscription?

Yes. A permanent desktop platform can provide teaching and media tools without adding another recurring software charge.
