Useful questions before you make the post.

Which social platforms can FlexCam publish to directly?

FlexCam can publish directly to connected YouTube and LinkedIn accounts after the normal platform connection is completed. It creates export-ready files for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What can I add to a FlexCam social post?

Add titles, captions, logos, taglines, stickers, entry and exit transitions, cropping and platform-specific safe-zone guides. Photo mode also lets you assemble images into a post.

Do I have to rebuild the project for every platform?

No. Build the source once, then preview and refine the framing for the destination formats you need.
