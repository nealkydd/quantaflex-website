How activation will work

Activation opens with the desktop release.

  1. Buy Quantaflex in the Quantaflex shop. Your order confirmation shows your order number and confirmation code.

  3. Activate. In Quantaflex, choose Activate Quantaflex and enter your order number (or confirmation code) and the email address you bought with.

Questions

Is Quantaflex a subscription?

No. Quantaflex is a single €9.50 payment and it does not expire, so there is nothing to renew. GitHub sponsorship is a separate, optional monthly route.

Is it mine to keep?

Yes. It is a one-time purchase: once activated, the three modules you chose stay unlocked for as long as you use Quantaflex. It never expires and is not a rental.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. Quantaflex is a desktop application for macOS and Windows, and you activate it with the email address you bought with.

Which modules can I choose?

Any three of the six: Kineplex, FlexCam, Cam2Cam, Pro Online Learning, Advanced Encoder and Media Inspector. You choose them in the app when you activate, and the choice is permanent.

Can I unlock the other modules later?

Yes. All six modules are visible in the app, and the ones you have not chosen are shown as locked. Upgrade options to unlock more modules are planned for November 2026.

Do I need to be online to use it?

Only once, to activate. After that, Quantaflex works without an internet connection.
