MEDIA INSPECTOR

Before you export,
know what is inside the file.

19 OCTOBER 2026 · QUANTAFLEX EDITORIAL

A video can look fine at first glance and still be the wrong source for the job. The file extension tells only part of the story. The useful decisions live inside it.

A file is more than its name.

Two files ending in .mov or .mp4 can behave very differently. Their container may be familiar, but the video codec, audio stream, bitrate, frame rate, pixel format and colour information may not be. Those details influence compatibility, the quality of the next encode, and how the media will play when it reaches another device.

That is why guessing is expensive. It can lead to an unnecessary conversion, the wrong delivery setting, a slow export, or a file that looks different after it is shared.

Start with a technical report.

Media Inspector gives you one clear report of the facts that matter: container, video and audio codecs, resolution, frame rate, bitrate, pixel format, colour information and stream details. It also lets you export that report when a collaborator, customer or support conversation needs the same evidence.

The point is not to turn every user into a broadcast engineer. It is to make the next step an informed one: keep the source as it is, prepare a compatible output, or choose an encoding setting with a reason behind it.

Clarity before conversion.

Inspect before you edit, encode or share. The work is faster when the file stops being a mystery.
