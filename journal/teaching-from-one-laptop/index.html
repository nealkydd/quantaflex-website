ONLINE TEACHING

Teach from one laptop
without losing your notes.

19 OCTOBER 2026 · QUANTAFLEX EDITORIAL

The learner needs a clean view of the material. The presenter needs notes, controls and a calm way to keep the session moving. Those are different jobs.

Give every person the right view.

When slides, notes and controls all compete in the same window, a lesson can begin to feel improvised. The learner sees too much, or the presenter loses the prompts that make the session flow.

Pro Online Learning extracts PowerPoint notes into a private presenter workspace. Learners see the shared slide; you retain the notes and session controls. It is a deliberate presenter view designed to work from one laptop, without the usual window-juggling.

It also presents PDFs and images cleanly. With a PDF, the learner sees the page rather than the rest of the desktop. With a PowerPoint deck, the session can stay focused on the material while you keep the lesson prompts private.

Prepare the first minute.

Bring in the deck, PDF or image material before the session. Review the opening slide and choose what learners need to see. A prepared first minute makes the rest of the lesson feel much easier.

Keep the conversation focused.

Use a background or blur the view behind you, share the material clearly, and keep the attention on the lesson. Quantaflex is built for more intimate group sessions and local desktop workflows—not another crowded workplace tool.
