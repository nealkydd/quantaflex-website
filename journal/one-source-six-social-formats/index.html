SOCIAL MEDIA

One source.
The right shape for every platform.

19 OCTOBER 2026 · QUANTAFLEX EDITORIAL

The hard part of a social post is usually not making the first version. It is rebuilding it for every place it needs to go.

The content is one idea. The canvases are not.

A wide YouTube video, a vertical Short, a square Instagram post and a LinkedIn update each ask for a different shape, crop and safe area. Starting again for every platform makes a simple campaign feel far more complicated than it should.

FlexCam lets you build the post from one video or photo source, then prepare versions for the platforms that matter. Add titles, captions, logos, taglines, stickers and transitions once. Use the safe-zone guides to keep the message where people will actually see it.

That means 1920 × 1080 for YouTube, 1080 × 1920 for Shorts and TikTok, 1080 × 1080 for Instagram, 1080 × 1350 for Facebook, and 1200 × 628 for LinkedIn. The creative idea remains one thing; the delivery can be precise.

Prepare clearly. Publish where it makes sense.

FlexCam can publish to connected YouTube and LinkedIn accounts. For TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, it creates correctly sized files ready for you to upload in the relevant app. That distinction matters: the point is a clean, usable asset for every channel—not a vague promise of automatic posting everywhere.

Keep the working files close.

Your social-video work is saved under Documents › Quantaflex Social Video. It remains your project, on your computer, ready to revisit when the next version or channel is needed.
